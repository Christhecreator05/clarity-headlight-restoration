How we restore a lens

  1. Mask

    We tape off the paint and trim so only the lens gets touched.

  2. Sand

    We wet sand through progressively finer grits, starting wherever your lens needs it, to remove the oxidized layer.

  3. Polish

    Polishing brings the lens back to optical clarity.

  4. Protect

    A vapor restoration and UV-protective finish seals the lens so it stays clear.
